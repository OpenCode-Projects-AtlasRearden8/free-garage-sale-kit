Host Guide — a friend to help you run your sale

Everything in this pack is a plain piece of paper: print it, fill it in, tape it, cross it out, spill coffee on it. Nothing here connects to anything. The PDFs are unbranded — no logos, no links, no credit lines — so slide them right into whatever else you use.

How to print the pack

  • PDFs are US Letter at 100% / “Actual size” — don't let the printer scale them down.
  • Signs (06) and labels (03) look best on a heavier stock; the rest is fine on normal paper.
  • The cards and labels are grid-aligned, so scissors or a paper trimmer both work well — the cuts are dashed, and eyes forgive a lot.

Rebuilding it if you ever want to

The HTML/CSS sources are in src/, and one script regenerates the whole printables/ folder:

  python3 build.py          # builds all PDFs into printables/ (fully offline)
  python3 build.py --list   # show the sheet list
  pytest                    # verifies every sheet builds

No internet connection, no accounts, no model calls, no cloud. The tests spin up the same local builder and validate every finished PDF.

Sale-day rhythm that tends to work

  • Doors open early; the haulers arrive 7 a.m. with coffee and opinions.
  • Tally everything on Sheet 8 as it happens — the price is on the card, the money is in the box.
  • Half off at noon; silent generosity collects smiles faster than perfect pricing collects dollars.
  • Take a two-minute layout photo before open; it will help set up next year's tables.
