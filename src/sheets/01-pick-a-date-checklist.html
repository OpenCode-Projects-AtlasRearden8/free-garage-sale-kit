Three weeks before

  • Pick the sale date and mark it on every calendar you use
  • Walk the house and label a donate pile too
  • Start filling tables one room at a time
  • Decide which rooms are off-limits (close the door)

Two weeks before

  • Price everything using the pricing cards or a marker
  • Gather tables, blankets, racks, hangers, extension cords
  • Fill the change fund (see the change-fund worksheet)
  • Arrange any help for sale morning

Week of the sale

  • Print signs and fill them in (Sheet 6)
  • Sketch your table layout on Sheet 5
  • Post it if your neighborhood allows it, walk the route and time it
  • Sort items by table and print Sheet 3 labels

Night before

  • Set up as much as possible tonight
  • Cash box counted, change fund worksheet done
  • Signs, tape, bags, pens, tally log in one tote
  • Early to bed — buyers arrive early

Sale morning

  • Signs up by 6:30–7:00 a.m., facing both directions
  • Doors, gates, and the path from street to tables are safe and clear
  • Tally log open on the payment table, first page ready
  • Take a photo of the layout before it gets busy (great for next time)
